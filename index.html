Helpful Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Quick information for families and individuals exploring rehabilitation and child development services in Tirupati.

What services does SVMM Rehabilitation Centre offer?

Short answer: SVMM offers physiotherapy, pediatric and neuro rehabilitation, occupational therapy, developmental therapy, speech therapy, special education, behavioral therapy, early intervention and sensory integration services.

When should I seek developmental support for my child?

If you have concerns about your child's movement, communication, play, learning or everyday skills, discuss them with a qualified healthcare professional. See the CDC developmental milestones guidance for general information.

What is pediatric physiotherapy?

Short answer: Pediatric physiotherapy is rehabilitation tailored to children to support movement, strength, balance, coordination and everyday participation, based on assessment and the child's needs.

What happens during a first therapy appointment?

Short answer: A first appointment usually begins with a discussion of concerns and goals, relevant history and current abilities, followed by an explanation of possible next steps. The process depends on the person and service.

Does SVMM provide occupational and speech therapy?

Short answer: Yes. Occupational therapy and speech therapy are among SVMM's child development services. Appropriate support is discussed after understanding the individual's needs and goals.

How can I book an appointment at SVMM in Tirupati?

Short answer: Call 9398237781 or 8340023505, or email info@svmmrehabcentre.com to ask about services or an assessment.
