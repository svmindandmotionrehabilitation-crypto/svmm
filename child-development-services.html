Child development · Tirupati

Support for children's development and participation.

What are child development services? Child development services support skills such as movement, communication, play, learning, sensory regulation and everyday participation. Support is selected according to the child's individual strengths, needs and family priorities.

SVMM brings several child-focused therapy services together. A conversation and assessment can help families understand available options and which professionals may be appropriate for their concerns.

Service areas

Which child development services are available?

Developmental therapy

Activities can be planned around functional goals in play, interaction, learning readiness and daily routines.

Occupational therapy

Depending on assessment, goals may include fine motor skills, self-care, participation in routines and sensory-related needs.

Speech therapy

Speech and language support may address communication, understanding, expression and functional interaction.

Special education

Individualized learning support can help build participation in learning and functional educational goals.

Behavioral therapy

Individualized approaches may support useful skills, routines and positive participation while respecting the child's needs.

Early intervention

Early support connects families with suitable strategies and services when developmental concerns are identified.

Sensory integration support

Activities and environmental strategies should be selected by trained professionals according to the child's profile and goals.

For families

What should parents do if they have a developmental concern?

Start by noting the skills or situations that concern you and discuss them with a qualified healthcare professional.

Write down what you notice

Record examples from everyday routines, including what your child finds easy, what is difficult and what support helps.

Ask about assessment and next steps

Share your observations and ask which assessment, referral or therapy options may fit your child's needs.

Keep goals practical and collaborative

Discuss goals that matter in daily life and how caregivers can support them safely between sessions. Progress differs for every child.

For general guidance, consult the World Health Organization's early child development resources and the CDC developmental milestones guidance. These sources do not diagnose an individual child.
