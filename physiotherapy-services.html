Physiotherapy focused on everyday function.

What is physiotherapy? Physiotherapy helps people work toward movement, function and participation goals through assessment, appropriate exercise, education and individualized rehabilitation planning.

SV Mind & Motion Rehabilitation Centre provides physiotherapy services for children and adults. The right approach depends on the person's history, current abilities, goals and recommendations from the treating professional.

Our focus areas

Which physiotherapy services can I ask about?

Discuss your needs with the centre so the team can explain which assessment or service may be appropriate.

Neuro rehabilitation

Rehabilitation goals may include mobility, balance, coordination, transfers and participation in daily activities, depending on the person's condition and care plan.

Pediatric physiotherapy

Child-focused physiotherapy may address movement skills, strength, balance, coordination and functional participation through age-appropriate activities.

Orthopedic rehabilitation

Rehabilitation after injury or musculoskeletal problems is planned around the diagnosis, healing stage, precautions and the treating clinician's guidance.

Post-surgical rehabilitation

Progression should follow the surgeon's instructions and an individualized protocol. Do not start or advance exercises without checking relevant precautions.

What to expect

How does a physiotherapy assessment work?

1. Discuss the concern and goals

Share the reason for seeking help, relevant medical history, current challenges and the activities that matter to you.

2. Understand current function

The clinician may assess movement and functional abilities appropriate to the referral and the person's tolerance.

3. Agree on next steps

Ask questions about goals, precautions, home activities, expected follow-up and when medical review is needed. Outcomes vary and cannot be guaranteed.

For general information about physical activity and health, see the World Health Organization's physical activity fact sheet. It is general information and not an individual treatment plan.
